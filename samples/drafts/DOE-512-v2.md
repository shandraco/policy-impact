# Student Devices

## 1 Purpose
1.1 This policy sets the rules for personal devices that students bring to school.

## 2 Phones during the school day
2.1 Students must store phones in lockers for the whole school day, including lunch.
2.2 Phones must be silent during lessons.

## 3 Searches
3.1 Staff must record every device search.
